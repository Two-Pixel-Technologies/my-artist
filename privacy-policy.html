Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

myartist.in ("we", "us", "our") connects people looking to hire event artists with performers and service providers who create profiles on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language and will be updated as our services, including future booking and payment features, evolve.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use myartist.in, we may collect:

  • Contact details you share with us, such as your name, phone number, email address and event requirements, when you submit an enquiry form or message us.
  • Profile information provided by artists signing up, including performance category, portfolio media, pricing and availability.
  • Basic usage information such as pages visited and device/browser type, used to keep the site working properly.
  • Any information you choose to share with us over WhatsApp, email or other communication channels linked from this site.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to enquiries, connect people hiring artists with suitable performers, improve our website and services, and communicate updates about myartist.in. If online payments or bookings are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to describe how payment-related information is handled.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant details between a person hiring an artist and the artist being enquired about, solely to facilitate that booking conversation. We may also share information with service providers who help us run the platform (for example, form or hosting providers), under obligations to keep it confidential.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is needed to respond to your enquiry, maintain artist profiles, or as required by law. You may request that we delete your information by contacting us.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update, correct or delete the personal information you have shared with us at any time by reaching out through our contact details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, particularly as new features such as bookings or payments are introduced. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Contact section of our website.
