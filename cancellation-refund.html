Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are approached for engagements arranged through myartist.in. It is intended as general guidance and will be updated once online bookings and payments are formally introduced on the platform.

Current Booking Process

At this time, myartist.in helps connect people hiring artists with performers through enquiry forms and direct communication (including WhatsApp). Any advance payments, deposits, cancellation charges or refund terms for a specific event are agreed directly between the person hiring the artist and the artist, and are not currently processed or held by myartist.in.

Cancelling an Enquiry

If you wish to cancel an enquiry submitted through our website before any arrangement has been confirmed with an artist, simply let us or the artist know as soon as possible. No fee applies to withdrawing an enquiry itself.

Cancelling a Confirmed Booking

Where a booking has been confirmed directly with an artist, cancellation terms (including any notice period, applicable charges or forfeited deposits) should be agreed upfront between the parties involved. We encourage both artists and the people hiring them to confirm these details in writing before finalising an event.

Refunds

We do not guarantee fixed refund timelines or amounts, since payments are not currently processed through myartist.in. Once an in-platform payment or booking feature is introduced, this section will be updated with clear timelines and eligibility criteria for refunds.

Disputes

If a disagreement arises between a person hiring an artist and the artist regarding a cancellation or refund, we encourage both parties to resolve it directly and in good faith. You are welcome to reach out to us for general guidance, though we are not able to guarantee a particular outcome.

Changes to This Policy

This Cancellation & Refund Policy may be revised as our services evolve, particularly with the introduction of online payments. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

For questions about a specific booking or this policy, please reach out via the Contact section of our website.
